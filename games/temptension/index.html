Temp Tension

Your life is time. Collect vanishing resources, craft tools, and survive enemies while every second keeps tightening the run.

Play on beingrkn.com Watch Trailer Play on itch.io

How To Play

Your life is time. If your lifetime reaches zero, the run ends. Resources do not stay forever, so collect wood and stone quickly. Your inventory also decays, so stored resources can disappear over time. To pick up wood or stone, first bring it inside your red range circle. Only resources inside your red range circle can be selected. Use the arrow keys to select a resource that is inside your red range circle. Press Space to claim the selected resource before it disappears. Collect wood and stone to craft tools like the axe and pickaxe. Tools help you automatically pick up resources inside your red range circle.

Controls

Move: WASD Select resource inside red circle: Arrow Keys Claim selected resource inside red circle: Space Open crafting/inventory: E Upgrade menu: Opens every 10 seconds

Enemies

Red Enemy: Moves toward you and shoots bullets. Touching it reduces your lifetime. Green Enemy: Aims, then dashes toward you. If it hits you, you lose lifetime. Purple Enemy: Creates a dangerous region around itself. If you enter its region, it steals lifetime from you.
